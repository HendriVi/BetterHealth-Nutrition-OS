# Evidence register — initial scope

This file defines the seed evidence model. It is not yet a validated clinical knowledge base.

## Grading

- **A:** clinical guidelines, replicated high-quality randomized evidence or established medication safety
- **B:** individual randomized trials, strong prospective evidence, robust clinical consensus or replicated observational evidence
- **C:** small trials, uncontrolled human studies or mechanistic human evidence
- **D:** preclinical, omics association or expert hypothesis
- **N-of-1:** reproducible individual response used only inside established safety constraints

## Seed entries

### KETO-PSY-001 — adjunctive ketogenic therapy in serious mental illness

Evidence: Shebani Sethi et al., *Ketogenic Diet Intervention on Metabolic and Psychiatric Health in Bipolar and Schizophrenia: A Pilot Trial*, Psychiatry Research, 2024.

Design: four-month, single-arm pilot in adults with schizophrenia or bipolar disorder and metabolic abnormalities.

Product use: supports structured feasibility, adherence, psychiatric outcome and metabolic outcome monitoring. It does not support claims of cure, automatic medication reduction or individual efficacy prediction.

Initial grade: C for psychiatric efficacy; B for feasibility and metabolic monitoring hypothesis generation.

### KETO-PSY-002 — multidisciplinary monitoring

Evidence base: adult ketogenic diet clinical practice literature and emerging psychiatric ketogenic consensus work.

Product use: requires a prescribing clinician, ketogenic-trained nutrition professional, baseline safety review, hydration and electrolyte planning, objective adherence measurement, adverse-event monitoring and stopping rules.

Initial grade: B, pending formal evidence appraisal and governance approval.

### SAFE-LI-002 — lithium, fluid and sodium stability

Product use: dietary transitions that materially alter fluid or sodium balance block ketogenic activation until the prescriber defines monitoring and patient instructions.

Initial grade: A medication-safety constraint. Exact implementation must be validated against current drug information and local clinical standards.

### WEAR-SLEEP-003 — consumer sleep measurements

Product use: repeated sleep duration and timing trends may inform low-risk plan adaptation. Consumer sleep-stage estimates cannot independently trigger clinical treatment decisions.

Initial grade: B for trend use, device-specific validation required.

### NUTR-ADEQ-001 — nutrient adequacy before optimization

Product use: the meal optimizer first satisfies configured nutrient, allergy, medication and disease constraints, then optimizes preference, cost, convenience and variety.

Initial grade: A as a product safety and dietetics principle; reference values remain jurisdiction- and population-specific.

## Evidence operations required

- Formal systematic search and appraisal protocol
- Named clinical reviewers
- Source document archive
- Citation and version control
- Scheduled surveillance for updated guidelines and safety information
- Conflict-of-interest declarations
- Withdrawal mechanism when evidence changes
