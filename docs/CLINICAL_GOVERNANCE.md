# Clinical governance and safety model

## Governance structure required before clinical use

- Psychiatric clinical lead
- Ketogenic-experienced dietitian or clinical nutrition lead
- Internal medicine / metabolic medicine advisor
- Clinical pharmacology or medication-safety reviewer
- Data-protection and information-security leads
- Medical-device regulatory specialist
- Patient representative

## Decision rule schema

Every active rule must contain:

- Unique identifier and version
- Intended clinical use
- Supported population
- Triggering data and required data quality
- Interpretation
- Intervention or workflow action
- Contraindications and exclusions
- Evidence grade and cited evidence
- Confidence and uncertainty statement
- Monitoring requirement
- Escalation or stopping condition
- Author, reviewer and approval dates
- Verification tests

## Safety classes

### Hard stop

Plan generation or protocol activation is blocked. Examples include unresolved medication safety, critical laboratory values, pregnancy-related restrictions, active eating-disorder risk or inability to maintain hydration.

### Immediate clinical review

The plan remains visible but cannot be modified or activated without review. Examples include meaningful renal or electrolyte changes, significant psychiatric deterioration or possible adverse drug effects.

### Advisory review

The system proposes a change with rationale and clinician review. Examples include persistent sleep irregularity, adherence decline or a repeated biomarker trajectory outside the treatment objective.

### Patient education

Low-risk explanatory information that does not alter treatment.

## Ketogenic module minimum controls

- Current psychiatric state and crisis risk
- Eating-disorder screening
- Pregnancy and lactation status
- Diabetes type, glucose-lowering drugs and SGLT2 inhibitor review
- Renal, hepatic, pancreatic, gallbladder and relevant metabolic history
- Medication review including lithium, diuretics, antihypertensives, insulin, sulfonylureas, valproate and topiramate
- Baseline laboratory and anthropometric configuration
- Prescriber and nutrition professional assigned
- Hydration and electrolyte plan
- Ketone and glucose measurement method
- Adverse-event and sick-day instructions
- Psychiatric outcome measurement schedule
- Repeat laboratory schedule
- Discontinuation and transition pathway

## Validation sequence

1. Rule verification against designed synthetic cases
2. Retrospective expert review using de-identified cases
3. Prospective silent-mode evaluation without influencing care
4. Clinician agreement and error analysis
5. Controlled pilot with formal adverse-event reporting
6. Clinical performance study supporting the final intended use
